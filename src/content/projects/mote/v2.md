## what changed

mana margherita was the name of our playgroup, and the app grew past it. in October 2026 the product became möte, and mana & margherita is now one playgroup that uses it.

the rewrite also changed these:

- **a new design system.** I redesigned the web, TV, and iPhone apps around it
- **native in place of Expo.** the Expo app is gone. a SwiftUI app with iOS 26 as the minimum replaced it, and it shares a MoteKit Swift package with the TV app
- **identity.** every player picks one of twelve pixel-art familiars, plus a realm, a glow, and lettering. player cards grow with the nights you've played, and I drew some signature cards by hand in Aseprite
- **a backend restructure.** playgroups own events, members, roles, and ratings, and Effect runs through the Convex functions

## features

- **playgroups.** invite codes, roles, and a picker for people in more than one
- **events.** RSVPs, date polls, and event suggestions
- **tournaments.** swiss pairings with rematch avoidance, Commander pods of 3 and 4, group stages, top cut, and custom formats
- **real tiebreakers.** OMW%, GW%, and OGW%, with the 33% floor from the WotC rules
- **synced timer.** the round clock comes from server timestamps, so every phone and the TV show the same time
- **ratings.** Elo per playgroup, which can seed the first round. OpenSkill ratings are next
- **table.** on iPhone, a shared game across phones with life totals, undo, and dice. in a duel, losing life makes the other player's familiar attack
- **honors and rewards.** first blood, comeback king, color master, champion, and mythic, plus reward cards with a fullscreen reveal
- **commander tools.** a bracket checker and share images for Commander lists and polls

## one backend, every screen

- **web** at [motehall.com](https://motehall.com/) is the full client for players and organizers
- **the tvOS app** is the room display. it shows the timer, pairings, standings, and the hall of player cards. it's read-only, so nobody changes a result from the couch
- **iPhone** has Tonight, Events, Table, and Players, with push notifications and a Live Activity for the current match

## tech stack

- Backend: Convex, Effect v4 with Confect, Better Auth
- Web: TanStack Start, React 19, Tailwind v4, shadcn with Base UI, Motion
- Apple: Swift 6, SwiftUI, iOS 26 and tvOS 26, a shared MoteKit Swift package
- Observability: OpenTelemetry to Axiom
- Hosting: Cloudflare Workers, Convex Cloud
- Tooling: Turborepo, pnpm, oxlint, oxfmt, Vitest, knip, Playwright

active development. web and tvOS are live, and the iOS app ships to testers through TestFlight.
