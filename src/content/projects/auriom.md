## what and why

I wanted somewhere to keep track of albums the way people track books and movies. auriom is that: log what you've listened to, rate and review albums, and see what the people you follow are into. I built it in 2022 during Buildspace Nights and Weekends S2.

## features

- **search** — find any album or artist, with catalog data from Spotify
- **reviews** — rate an album and write about it if you want to
- **listening status** — mark albums as listened, listening, or want to listen
- **shelves** — make your own named collections of albums, public or private
- **profiles and follows** — follow people and see their reviews and shelves

## tech stack

- Framework: Next.js and React, started from create-t3-app
- API: tRPC with TanStack Query
- Data: Prisma, hosted on Railway
- Auth: Clerk
- Styling: Tailwind
- Music data: the Spotify Web API
