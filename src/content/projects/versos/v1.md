## what and why

the first version of the idea: paste a tracklist from a DJ set, get a Spotify playlist back. everything, from parsing to matching, lived in one Next.js app.

## how it works

1. **Sign in with Spotify**
   - Lucia handles sessions, Spotify OAuth grants playlist access

2. **LLM parse**
   - the raw tracklist goes through the AI SDK's `generateObject` with a zod schema
   - I swapped between GPT-3.5, Claude 3 Haiku, and Llama on Groq to compare speed and quality

3. **Embed**
   - each parsed track is embedded locally with transformers.js
   - embeddings are cached in an LRU so repeat tracks skip the model

4. **Vector search**
   - Postgres with pgvector finds the nearest tracks by cosine similarity
   - each result gets a high, medium, or low confidence, and low ones are flagged for review

5. **Playlist**
   - matches are looked up on Spotify and the accepted ones become a playlist

## tech stack

- Next.js, tRPC, React Query, Tailwind
- Drizzle, libSQL for app data, Postgres + pgvector for embeddings
- Lucia and Arctic for auth
- AI SDK with OpenAI, Anthropic, and Groq
- transformers.js for embeddings
